The Symmetry of Resonance

The Three Pillars & The Three Fractures

✦ The Three Pillars
✧ The Three Fractures
Unity

Resonance of fullness choosing fullness — standing beside kin and nature at a shared table without hierarchy or lack.

Severance

The primordial cutting of oneself from the shared table — marking a line between kin and stranger until walls and swords follow.

Liberty

Voluntary closeness and sacred boundaries — entering only where the door is opened, and honoring the unbent nature of every soul.

Coercion

Trespassing upon another’s dwelling, will, or nature — forcing a whole soul to bend through law, shame, or contrived scarcity.

Clarity

Transparent truth and courageous doubt — looking plainly at every boundary to ask who drew it, why, and whether it is true.

Dishonesty

Distortion, slander, and false piety — wearing a mask of lies before the Maker or blackening a neighbor’s name with words forged of breath.
